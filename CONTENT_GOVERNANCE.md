# Beyond Financials - Content Governance & Communication Guidelines

**Status**: Active Operational Policy  
**Applies To**: All public website pages, documentation, metadata, API communications, and early-access collateral for `beyondfinancials.ng`.

---

## 1. Core Communication Philosophy

Beyond Financials communicates with **clarity, technical precision, transparency, and dignity**. We reject deceptive marketing, high-pressure countdowns, fabricated metrics, and predatory financial jargon.

Every piece of public copy must be:
1. **Factually Grounded**: Directly traceable to real architecture, active Phase 1 engineering deliverables, or documented roadmap milestones.
2. **Pre-Launch Transparent**: Clear that services are currently in engineering and not yet live for consumer deposits or withdrawals.
3. **Respectful & Ethical**: Non-judgmental language regarding credit, savings habits, and financial situations.

---

## 2. Permitted vs. Prohibited Language Rules

### A. Regulatory & Entity Classification
- **APPROVED**:
  - "Beyond Financials is a digital financial technology platform."
  - "Banking services and virtual accounts will be provided in direct partnership with licensed financial institutions."
  - "Designed with reference to Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) regulatory tiers and Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) principles."
  - "Investment features are part of our future roadmap in collaboration with SEC-licensed capital market operators."
- **STRICTLY PROHIBITED**:
  - Do NOT claim Beyond Financials is a "licensed commercial bank" or "CBN-licensed bank".
  - Do NOT claim Beyond Financials is "CBN-certified", "SEC-approved", or "NDIC-insured" as an individual standalone company without confirmed partner agreements.
  - Do NOT claim official government endorsement or partnership unless authorized.

### B. Financial Returns & Underwriting
- **APPROVED**:
  - "Disciplined savings with transparent terms."
  - "Transparent, capped interest rates without hidden deductions."
  - "Ethical credit underwriting subject to affordability and identity checks."
  - "All investments carry market risk. We do not promise speculative or guaranteed returns."
- **STRICTLY PROHIBITED**:
  - Do NOT use "guaranteed returns", "guaranteed profit", "risk-free wealth", or "double your money".
  - Do NOT promise "100% loan approval for everyone" or "no-questions-asked cash".
  - Do NOT invent specific annual percentage yields (e.g. "Earn 25% APY guaranteed") before partner agreements are finalized.

### C. Security & Data Protection
- **APPROVED**:
  - "Server-side secret encapsulation with zero client-side credentials."
  - "Immutable double-entry ledger architecture for auditable reconciliation."
  - "Strict role-based access control and PII redaction."
  - "Zero contact list harvesting or predatory privacy violations."
- **STRICTLY PROHIBITED**:
  - Do NOT use "military-grade security", "bank-grade encryption", "unhackable", or "100% impenetrable".
  - Do NOT claim third-party certifications (e.g. "ISO 27001 certified", "SOC 2 Type II certified", "PCI-DSS certified") unless formal audits have been executed and verified.

### D. Historical & Traction Metrics
- **APPROVED**:
  - "Join our growing community of early access members."
  - "Contracted Phase 1 engineering roadmap."
  - "Built for individuals, salary earners, entrepreneurs, and businesses across Nigeria."
- **STRICTLY PROHIBITED**:
  - Do NOT invent fake customer counts (e.g. "Trusted by 2.5 million Nigerians").
  - Do NOT invent fake transaction volumes (e.g. "Processed ₦50 billion in volume").
  - Do NOT invent fake corporate awards, venture capital funding announcements, or press logos.
  - Do NOT invent executive names or biographies unless explicitly supplied by the client.

## 3. Standard Service Status Taxonomies

To maintain clarity across all product pages and navigation:

| Status Badge | Meaning | Standard Customer Explanation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Phase 1 Priority** / **Phase 1 Deliverable** | Active development milestone currently in sprint execution. | Core infrastructure being built for the initial onboarding release. |
| **Early Access Waitlist** | Service is in pre-launch registration mode. | Visitors can register their contact details to receive priority onboarding invitations. |
| **Future Strategic Scope** / **Phase 2 Roadmap** | Planned future expansion (Investments, Crypto, International). | Transparently communicated as future releases that will deploy under appropriate regulatory frameworks. |

---

## 4. Contact & Data Collection Ethics
1. Forms must only collect **purpose-specific, non-sensitive information** (Name, Email, Optional Phone Number, Category, Message).
2. All contact interfaces must display a prominent security notice:  
   *"Beyond Financials will never ask for your BVN, NIN, password, card PIN, or cryptographic recovery phrase. Never share financial secrets in general contact forms."*
3. Form submissions must be processed via server-side endpoints with validation and error sanitization.
